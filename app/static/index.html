CA-Net Architectural Blueprint

1
Backbone: DenseNet-121

Extracts deep hierarchical features F_in ∈ ℝ^(7×7×1024) from retinal images resized to 224×224.

2
Triplet Channel Attention Block (TCAB)

Simultaneously captures 1D Channel weights, 2D Channel-Width cross-attention (CHAP over H), and 2D Channel-Height cross-attention (CWAP over W) merged via learnable scalar weights: F_ch = w₁F₁ + w₂F₂ + w₃F₃.

3
Spatial Attention Block (SAB)

Performs Cross-Channel Average Pooling (CCAP) over K channels followed by 1×1 Conv, LayerNorm, and Sigmoid to highlight lesion regions: F_sp = σ(Conv(V)) ⊗ F_ch.

4
Classifier Head & Explainability

GAP layer followed by 3-neuron FC layer with Softmax for Normal, Early, and Advanced stages. Interpreted via Grad-CAM and Grad-CAM++.
